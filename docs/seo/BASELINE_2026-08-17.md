## 1. Headline verdicts

1. **torro.cat is invisible.** Absent from every one of ~40 queries tested across
   Catalan, Spanish and English — including the exact brand query `torrorèndum` and
   `site:torro.cat`. Until indexation is fixed and verified (Search Console, Bing,
   Brave), nothing else matters. This is the P0 blocker.
2. **The interactive lane is empty.** No persistent voting/bracket/ELO turrón product
   exists anywhere in the observed index. Torrorèndum is category-of-one — but that
   also means zero native search demand for "vote on torró"; demand must be captured
   through informational/ranking queries and press, then converted into the loop.
3. **The most winnable high-intent queries are exactly our product**: `rànquing
   torrons` (emptiest Catalan SERP tested), `ranking turrones` (weakest Spanish head
   SERP — a TierMaker tier list ranks #1), `quin és el millor torró`, `millors
   torrons Vicens` / `qué turrón Vicens comprar` (no answer page exists anywhere).
4. **OCU owns Spanish "best turrón" semantics** through an annual December lab test
   whose full results are paywalled and whose free press note is rewritten by dozens
   of outlets (ES and CA). AI assistants recite OCU verdicts today. The exploit:
   we can be the only *free, open, continuously-updated, per-product* dataset — the
   complement ("experts test; the crowd votes"), not the challenger.
5. **Catalan competition is thin**: one strong listicle publisher (elnacional.cat
   Gourmeteria), OCU rewrites, institutional pages, and a self-interested affiliate
   (turronesydulces.com/cat) that ranks #2 for `millors torrons` while selling what
   it rates. A focused, honest, data-backed Catalan site can own this cluster in one
   season.
6. **Year-labeled seasonal queries are first-mover**: nobody has published "torrons
   Nadal 2026" content yet (August). Publishing the seasonal hub in Sept–Oct meets
   zero year-labeled competition.

### On-site state (repo, after this run's fixes)

The July audit's P0 (robots/sitemap/llms 404 bug) was verified fixed. This run's
audit found **no P0**, one P1 (images — now fixed), and a P2/P3 tail (mostly now
fixed). State after this run's two implementation commits:

- **Indexable surface:** `/` · `/ranquing-de-torrons` (NEW — public, stable,
  crawlable community ranking with ItemList schema; the personalized `/leaderboard`
  stays noindex) · `/classes` · `/torro/{id}` ×107 (now with Product schema +
  product-photo og:image) · `/sobre` (FAQPage) · `/torro-agramunt-igp` +
  `/torro-agramunt-vs-xixona` (Article, now dated) · `/tipus-de-torrons`
  (DefinedTermSet) · `/premsa` (Dataset, now CC BY 4.0-licensed) · `/advent` ·
  `/bracket/{classId}` (now per-class titles). Personal/randomized pages correctly
  noindex. No orphans; footer links the whole cluster.
- **Season year is dynamic** (`seasonYear` template func) — titles/descriptions say
  2026 now and roll over automatically (January keeps the prior season's year).
- **robots.txt** allows everything, explicitly welcomes 13 AI crawler UAs, disallows
  `/api/`, points at the sitemap. **llms.txt** served with the independence
  disclosure. **Sitemap** covers all indexable routes (verified zero mismatch).
- **Performance:** product images 19 MB → 3 MB (the audit's #1 CWV finding); htmx
  self-hosted with `defer` (was render-blocking unpkg, a known SPOF); gzip on;
  tiered cache headers; security headers + HSTS. Remaining perf backlog: Google
  Fonts still render-blocking third-party (P3), 194 KB CSS (P3).
- **Correctness:** branded error page now exists (was a dead reference in every
  handler), branded 404 with recovery links, trailing-slash 301s.
- **Deliberately absent:** aggregateRating/star markup anywhere (ELO is not a
  review scale; policy + legal risk), `offers` on Product (not a shop), hreflang
  (single-language until `/es/` ships).

**Deploy freshness is unverified** — if production predates this branch, none of the
above is live. First check next run: does the live sitemap contain
`/ranquing-de-torrons`?

## 3. Competition status (snapshot)

Power map (detail + URLs in `research/competitors.md`, `research/serp-landscape-*.md`):

| Actor | Owns | Weakness we exploit |
|---|---|---|
| **OCU** (ocu.org) | "mejor turrón (del supermercado)", "según la OCU" trust anchor; media echo every December | Paywalled detail; supermarket-only scope; no Catalan depth; no community data; annual-only |
| **Directo al Paladar** | Expert catas, "quién fabrica" evergreen | One palate, ~10 products, static, Spanish-only |
| **elnacional.cat Gourmeteria** | Strongest Catalan listicles | Annual, no data, no interactivity |
| **OCU-echo ring** (Infobae, okdiario, e-notícies, NacióDigital, vadegust, catalunyapress…) | Long-tail "mejor turrón…" via freshness | Zero original data — they need sources; they are our future *distributors* |
| **turronesydulces.com** (+/cat) | Mid-tail commercial queries, ranks #2 for `millors torrons` | Thin, self-interested (sells what it ranks) — beatable on E-E-A-T |
| **Brand publishers** (vicens.com, Xixona brands, madeinjijona.com) | Brand/navigational + some educational tails | Can't rank/compare their own products; Vicens does no informational SEO; seasonal shop-closure snippet observed ("Tienda cerrada temporalmente") |
| **Institutions** (gencat/gastroteca, Viquipèdia, IGP councils, barcelona.cat) | Definitional queries (Agramunt, Xixona, IGP) | Not competitors — citation allies; go long-tail around them |
| **AI-spam affiliates** (culturavalenciana.es, cumul.es, lorcaalacarta.es…) | Uncontested mid-tails incl. "turrones Vicens análisis" | Zero E-E-A-T; displaceable by any real page |
| **UGC** (TierMaker tier list, TikTok/IG catas) | "ranking turrones" #1! | Proves demand for participatory ranking; none is a real product |
| **Interactive competitors** | — | **None exist** (verified by multiple negative searches) |
